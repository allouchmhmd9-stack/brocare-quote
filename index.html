Prime — Full hospital network and the highest limits. Nothing excluded, except Bellevue on Class SP.

Smart Plus — Keeps AUBMC. Excludes CMC, Rizk, Saint John and Bellevue.

Smart — Same as Smart Plus but also drops AUBMC, and prosthesis limits are lower.

Essential — Smallest network: also excludes KMC. Class SP only. Bariatric surgery not covered, and a $50 co-pay on emergencies not followed by admission.

A / B / SP is the hospitalisation class and sets the money limits — organ transplant is covered to $60,000 / $40,000 / $20,000 a year respectively. Prime · Smart Plus · Smart · Essential set which hospitals you can use.
